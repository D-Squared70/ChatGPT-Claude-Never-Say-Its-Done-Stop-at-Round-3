The review prompt

Review this letter to our customers about our price increase. The customers need to understand the new price, when it starts, and what they should do next.

For this letter, a real problem is: a wrong number or date, anything a customer could easily misread, or a missing next step.

List every issue you find. For each one, quote the exact sentence and label it MUST FIX or OPTIONAL.

Start with how many MUST FIX issues there are. If there are none, say “Nothing must be fixed.”
